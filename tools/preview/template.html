Time font proposals

Each face shows the hours and minutes in a different font, rendered at the watch's real 260 px with only the 64 MIP colours. Every option is fitted to today's space: digits at most 47 px tall, and the widest possible time at most 127 px wide, so the week number still clears the side panel. The seconds and week number stay as they are.

Round two. Your picks A6, B4 and D2 are kept as they were. The dot matrix is redone after your reference: pick one of C5, C6 or C7. Use the buttons to try other times. Widest shows each font's widest time.
